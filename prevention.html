12 · Prevention

Primary prevention reduces risk

Not smoking tobacco is the best way to prevent lung cancer. Quitting lowers risk — even after many years of smoking.

Prevention stops the disease from occurring; screening finds existing cancer early (next slide).

01

Don't start smoking; seek help to quit (counselling, medicines, nicotine replacement)

02

Avoid secondhand smoke; support smoke-free environments

03

Test homes for radon and fix high levels

04

Protect against workplace carcinogens (e.g. asbestos) — follow safety precautions

05

Reduce air pollution; tobacco-control policies

06

Diet rich in fruit and vegetables; regular exercise; avoid high-dose vitamin pills

Sources: WHO (n.d.); Mayo Clinic (n.d.-a).
