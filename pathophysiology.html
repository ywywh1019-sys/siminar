04 · Causes & Pathophysiology

From carcinogen exposure to metastasis

Risk factors describe who is more likely to develop the disease; the cause is a biological process of accumulated DNA damage.

1

Exposure

Inhaled carcinogens (e.g. in cigarette smoke) damage cells lining the lungs.

2

Repeated injury

The body repairs early damage, but repeated exposure overwhelms repair.

3

DNA changes

Altered genes tell cells to multiply quickly and to survive past their normal lifespan.

4

Tumor

Excess cells form a mass that invades and destroys healthy tissue.

5

Metastasis

Cells break away and spread to distant organs (metastatic cancer).

Sources: Mayo Clinic (n.d.-a); Cleveland Clinic (n.d.).
