09 · Diagnosis

Stepwise diagnostic and staging workup

Imaging raises suspicion — tissue examination confirms the diagnosis and type.

1

Step 01

History & exam

Symptoms, smoking history, physical examination.

2

Step 02

Imaging

Chest X-ray, CT, MRI or PET show location and size.

3

Step 03

Tissue sampling

Sputum cytology; biopsy by bronchoscopy, mediastinoscopy (lymph nodes) or CT-guided needle.

4

Step 04

Staging

MRI, CT, bone scan, PET look for spread to nodes and organs.

5

Step 05

Molecular tests

Gene changes / biomarkers that guide targeted treatment.

Sources: WHO (n.d.); Mayo Clinic (n.d.-b).
