11 · Nursing Management

Evidence-informed nursing interventions

Breathlessness

  • Pursed-lip and diaphragmatic breathing
  • Positioning — leaning forward may help
  • Pacing activity; saving energy for priorities
  • Fan (air-flow) therapy
  • Oxygen and medicines as prescribed

Psychosocial support

  • Relaxation to reduce fear and anxiety that worsen dyspnea
  • Encourage family, counselling and support groups
  • Nurse-led follow-up reduces breathlessness and distress

Education & safety

  • Smoking-cessation support and resources
  • Report worsening breathlessness, pain or new symptoms
  • Help patients prepare questions and understand their treatment options

Sources: Yates et al. (2013); Kako et al. (2023); Mayo Clinic (n.d.-a, n.d.-b).
