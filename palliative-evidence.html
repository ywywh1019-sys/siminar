Early palliative care improves outcomes

Supportive and palliative care relieve pain and symptoms and improve quality of life for patients and families at all stages, given at the same time as surgery, chemotherapy or radiation.

(WHO, n.d.; Mayo Clinic, n.d.-b)

Evidence: randomized trial of 151 patients with newly diagnosed metastatic NSCLC — early palliative care plus standard oncology care vs. standard care alone.
