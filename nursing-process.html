Nursing process in lung cancer

PhaseNursing focus
AssessmentBreathlessness, cough, hemoptysis, pain, fatigue, appetite and weight, anxiety and mood, warning signs (e.g. facial/neck swelling)
Nursing diagnosesSelect only when assessment findings support them — e.g. problems of breathing, pain, fatigue, anxiety, knowledge needs*
PlanningPatient-centred goals agreed with the patient, family and multidisciplinary team
ImplementationSymptom management, psychosocial support, education, safe care during treatment
EvaluationReassess symptoms against goals; escalate deterioration promptly

*Formal diagnosis labels should be verified against a current approved nursing classification (e.g. NANDA-I); no such reference was verified for this seminar.

Sources: Mayo Clinic (n.d.-a); Yates et al. (2013).
