12 · Prevention — Early Detection

LDCT screening: high-risk adults only

U.S. Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF) recommends yearly LDCT for people who meet all three criteria:

20+

pack-year smoking history

≤15

currently smoke, or quit within the past 15 years

50–80

years of age

Benefit

Finding cancer early, when treatment works best, can reduce deaths in high-risk people.

Risks

False-positive results, overdiagnosis, and radiation from repeated scans. Not recommended for everyone.

Sources: CDC (2026b), summarizing USPSTF guidance; WHO (n.d.). Eligibility reflects U.S. guidance.
