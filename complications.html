08 · Complications

Local effects, emergencies and spread

Airway obstruction

Tumor blocking the major airways causes shortness of breath.

Pleural effusion & lung collapse

Fluid in the pleural space limits lung expansion and can push air out so the lung collapses; it can be drained.

Hemoptysis

Bleeding in the airway; sometimes severe — treatments are available to control it.

Superior vena cava syndrome

Tumor pressing on the superior vena cava may cause facial swelling and swollen neck veins.

Metastatic complications

Spread to brain, bones, lymph nodes, liver or adrenal glands may cause pain, nausea or headaches depending on the organ.

Pain

Spread to the lung lining or bone can cause pain; many treatments can control it.

Sources: Mayo Clinic (n.d.-a); Cleveland Clinic (n.d.).
