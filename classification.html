05 · Classification

Two histological types: NSCLC and SCLC

Classified by how the cells look under a microscope — the type guides treatment decisions.

~85%

Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)

  • The most common type — around 85% of cases
  • A category including several subtypes:
    adenocarcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, large cell carcinoma
  • Staged with the TNM system (stages I–IV)

SCLC

Small Cell Lung Cancer (SCLC)

  • Less common, but typically more aggressive
  • Usually occurs in people with a long history of heavy smoking
  • Often described as limited or extensive stage

Sources: WHO (n.d.); Mayo Clinic (n.d.-a, n.d.-b).
