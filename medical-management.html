10 · Medical Management

Treatment is individualized — not every patient receives every option

Choice depends on cancer type, stage, molecular profile, overall health and the patient's preferences.

SurgeryWedge resection, segmentectomy, lobectomy or pneumonectomy for cancer confined to the lung; lymph nodes may be removed for testing.
RadiotherapyBefore/after surgery or with chemotherapy; stereotactic body radiotherapy for small tumors when surgery isn't possible; symptom relief in spread disease.
ChemotherapyBefore surgery to shrink, after surgery to kill remaining cells, with radiation, or to relieve symptoms.
Targeted therapyWorks only when tumor cells carry specific DNA changes identified by lab testing.
ImmunotherapyHelps the immune system find and kill cancer cells — after surgery or when surgery isn't an option.

Sources: WHO (n.d.); Mayo Clinic (n.d.-b).
