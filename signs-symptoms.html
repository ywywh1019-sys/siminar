07 · Signs & Symptoms

Often silent early; varied when advanced

Lung cancer typically causes no symptoms early on; early symptoms may be mild or mistaken for common respiratory problems. Presentation varies between patients.

Respiratory

New cough that doesn't go away, chest pain, coughing up blood, shortness of breath, wheezing, hoarseness, recurrent lung infections

Systemic

Fatigue, unexplained weight loss, loss of appetite

Advanced / spread

Bone pain, headache, swelling in the face or neck

Sources: WHO (n.d.); Mayo Clinic (n.d.-a).
