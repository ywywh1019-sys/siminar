01 · Introduction

The world's leading cause of cancer death

  • Lung cancer is a major public-health challenge because of its high incidence and mortality.
  • It is often diagnosed at advanced stages, when treatment options are limited — early recognition matters.
  • Treatment depends on type, stage, molecular profile and health status; multidisciplinary care is essential.
  • Nurses support patients across screening, diagnosis, treatment, symptom control and palliative care.

Global burden · 2022 estimates

2.5M

new cases — about 1 in 8 cancers diagnosed worldwide (12.4%)

1.8M

deaths — 18.7% of all cancer deaths, the highest of any cancer

Sources: WHO (n.d.); Bray et al. (2024), GLOBOCAN 2022 estimates for 185 countries.
