02 · Definition

Abnormal lung cells that grow without control

Lung cancer occurs when abnormal cells grow uncontrollably in the lungs, forming a malignant growth that can spread beyond its origin.

1  Malignant growth

DNA changes make cells multiply quickly and survive when healthy cells would die, forming a tumor.

2  Local invasion

The tumor can grow to invade and destroy nearby healthy tissue.

3  Metastasis

Cells break away and spread — commonly to the brain and bones; also lymph nodes, liver and adrenal glands.
